# Public transcript normalization

The generated evidence in this public release is semantically identical to the
fresh replay packet, with two deterministic privacy normalizations:

1. the replay-machine hostname in `generated/environment.txt` is replaced by
   `<redacted-host>`; and
2. the author workstation prefix in representative AddressSanitizer stack
   traces is replaced by `<research-root>`.

No status code, source filename, source line, diagnostic category, aggregate,
test record, cryptographic value, or program result was changed. The original
pre-normalization receipt is preserved as
`ORIGINAL_VALIDATION_RECEIPT.json`; its SHA-256 is
`a6cfe02bca804f00f6c944df719691dc15e2292b0f669859209d1bed4a186609`.

`generated/validation_receipt.json` is the authority for the public normalized
packet and is regenerated by `validate_results.py`.
